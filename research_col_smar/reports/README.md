The human-readable layer of the NSB-96-UPGRADE package: the aggregate
verdict tables of the thirteen laboratories that stress the b-correction
program at doubled resolution (96³, 112³) and audit the companion
φ-attractor monograph independently. Every table here is generated by a
tool in [`tools/`](../tools/README.md) from a pinned record in
[`results/results/`](../reference/README.md) — the documents quote the
records, never the other way round.

## On the two losses

The two L17 losses are the package's honesty marker, not a weakness of
the core program: the audit found that the φ-monograph's oscillation
formulas (D.5b)–(D.8) describe a *different configuration class* (rigid
rotators) than the two-parabolic geometry of its §2, and the corrected
life-time estimate — `τ_φ ≈ 4908` turnover times, ~15× the previously
stated value — **strengthens** the monograph's longevity conclusion. The
corrections are published verbatim with reproduction commands in
[`PHI_FORMULA_FIXES.md`](PHI_FORMULA_FIXES.md) and cross-checked
Julia↔Python (24/24 PASS, drift `1.9×10⁻¹⁴`).

## Reproduce everything

```bash
python3 research_col_smar/tools/nsb_extra_research.py --run all   # L11–L17 (~10 min)
python3 research_col_smar/tools/nsb_extra_research.py --run 17    # the φ-audit (~3 s)
julia   research_col_smar/tools/nsb_extra_research.jl phi17       # the Julia cross-check
bash    research_col_smar/tools/nsb_lab.sh                        # the main suite L1–L8
```
