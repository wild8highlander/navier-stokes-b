The figure set of the **NSB-96-UPGRADE chapters** (chapter 15 of the
constants monograph and §16.29 of the KdV chapter), generated by
[`tools/nsb_extra_research.py`](../../tools/README.md) and
[`tools/nsb_lab.py`](../../tools/README.md) at 600 dpi in two language
editions each. Every panel is driven by a pinned record in
[`results/results/`](../../results/results/README.md).

## Conventions

- Language suffixes: `_en` / `_ru` — the two editions use identical data,
  identical layout, translated captions.
- Everything at 600 dpi; the older 300-dpi figure families of the
  satellite's core program are in
  [`figures/en/`](../en/README.md) and [`figures/ru/`](../ru/README.md).
- Regeneration: `python3 research_col_smar/tools/nsb_extra_research.py
  --run figures` (and the L9 lab for the main suite).
