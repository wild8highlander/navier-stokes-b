What is this?

NSB Web Lab is the browser edition of the polyglot Navier–Stokes b-Lab — a research toy that verifies, numerically and honestly, what the “b-correction” hypothesis from the navier-stokes-b repository can and cannot do. The lab’s verdict: a full lattice symmetry is just a relabeling of degrees of freedom and cannot affect uniqueness or regularity, while a pointwise rotation preserves energy but destroys incompressibility (div u = 0) — so it cannot regularize the Euler equations either.

The physics inside

The 2-D tab integrates the barotropic vorticity equation on a β-plane with a pseudospectral method: de-aliased FFTs (2/3 rule), exact spectral derivatives and RK4 time stepping. The 3-D tab runs a compact Taylor–Green decay on the torus with Leray projection applied at every RK4 sub-stage — the same solver core as the Julia/Python/C/C++/Rust/Go/PHP editions in this repository.

∂ω/∂t + J(ψ, ω) + β ∂ψ/∂x = ν ∇²ω
  • BKM monitor: sup|ω|, enstrophy Ω, palinstrophy P and the BKM integral ∫ sup|ω| dt are tracked live.
  • K41: the shell spectrum E(k) is fitted against Kolmogorov’s −5/3 slope in the inertial range.
  • Honest numbers: every diagnostic is computed with Parseval identities — no tuning, no hidden rescaling.

Eight editions, one physics

This page is one of eight ports of the same laboratory. Every edition passes the identical self-test suite: FFT round-trips at machine precision, measured RK4 order ≈ 4, machine-level divergence after Leray projection, and the relabeling/isometry audit.

How to read the verdicts

Each CLI experiment prints PASS/FAIL checks with the measured numbers and their thresholds. The scope note matters: a verdict is a certificate of internal consistency of the computed solution within the computed window — it is evidence, not a general theorem. The full write-up (equations, proofs, benchmarks, Termux walk-through) lives in the repository README.
