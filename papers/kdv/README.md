The integrable continuation of the program: the same constant
`b = 1/(4π + 2√3)` surfacing in soliton interactions of the Korteweg–de
Vries equation. This is the strongest hint that the geometry is not an
artifact of one system — the identical closed form governs the phase
bookkeeping of two-soliton collisions, and Section 4 of the
[verification framework](../../verification/README.md) makes that claim
executable.
